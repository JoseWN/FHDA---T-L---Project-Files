The Accounting Equation

Assets = Liabilities + Equity
This equation MUST always balance — after every transaction, at every moment.
Expanded: Assets = Liabilities + Capital + Revenues − Expenses − Withdrawals
Assets

Resources owned or controlled. Future economic benefit. Current (cash, A/R, supplies) + Non-current (equipment, intangibles).

→ Recorded at HISTORICAL COST

Liabilities

Present obligations to outside parties. Current (A/P, wages payable, unearned revenue) + Long-term (notes payable, bonds).

→ Unearned Revenue = LIABILITY (not yet earned)

Equity

Residual interest = Assets − Liabilities. Increases with revenues; decreases with expenses and withdrawals.

→ Revenue ≠ Cash | Expense ≠ Cash Paid

3-Question Transaction Framework

①
Which accounts are affected?
②
By how much and in which direction?
③
Does the equation still balance?
⚠️ Common Errors: Revenue ≠ Cash received. Collecting A/R = asset swap (no new revenue). Withdrawals ≠ expense. Advance received = liability (Unearned Revenue).

Key Terms

Business Transaction — economic event that changes financial position; measurable in dollars
Entity Assumption — business is separate from owner; no personal transactions in business records
Monetary Unit — only events measurable in dollars are recorded
Cost Principle — assets recorded at original acquisition cost, not market value
Revenue — increases equity; recognized when EARNED, not when cash is received
Matching Principle — expenses recognized same period as revenues they helped generate
Unearned Revenue — cash received before service delivered; a LIABILITY
Normal Balance — side of T-account on which increases are recorded
Chart of Accounts — numbered master list of all business accounts
Source Document — original evidence that a transaction occurred; basis for every entry

⚠️ Ethics: The Fraud Triangle

🔥
Pressure
Financial stress, revenue targets, bonus tied to results, fear of bad news
🔓
Opportunity
Weak controls, one person with too much access, no oversight or review
💭
Rationalization
"I'll fix it next quarter" / "It's temporary" / "Everyone does it"
Remember: Every accounting fraud violates the equation. The equation is not just math — it is an integrity constraint. Recording a false entry requires breaking A = L + E, then concealing that break with another false entry.
