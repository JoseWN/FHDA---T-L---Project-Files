• Purpose and Focus: Strategic decision-making is essential for the highest levels of management. Technology planning involves evaluating a company's goals and objectives and determining the right technology to reach them. • Formality and Internal Use: Strategic goals require formalization. For example, a company’s security strategy must begin with creating formal written information security policies to establish standards and provide a basis for enforcement. Company-wide enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems, which bring together human resources, operations, and technology, are an integral part of business strategy.
• Key Concept for a Learner: A strategic plan is the formal, guiding document that ensures technology investments (IT assets) and organizational knowledge are aligned to maximize competitive advantage and return on investment (ROI). It addresses foundational issues like security, system design, and the use of management information systems (MIS).
Blog
A blog is an external, public, and often collaborative platform primarily used in the context of online marketing, advertising, and monitoring public opinion.
• Purpose and Content: Blogs are classified as third-party sites. They are used as a platform for contextual advertising (such as Google AdSense), where PPC ads reach readers based on the content of the page. Individual bloggers can generate revenue by hosting these ads, especially if the site has significant readership.
• Marketing Function: Blogs are critical to viral marketing, which spreads brand awareness and is considered a Web 2.0 version of word-of-mouth promotion.
• Impact on Business (Reputation): The public discussion found in the blogosphere can have a severe negative impact on a business, as demonstrated by the "Dell Hell" rants concerning poor products and service. Companies must engage in social media monitoring to keep watch on external messages, including those on blogs, to identify problems and opportunities.
• Key Concept for a Learner: A blog is a crucial component of the modern online environment, serving as both a revenue stream via advertising and a volatile forum for public opinion that businesses must actively monitor and engage with.
